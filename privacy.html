Privacy Policy

Parapet for Mac. Last updated 27 September 2026.

Parapet collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and nothing you do in Safari or in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Safari extensions

  • The Safari extensions have no network access and never see the pages you visit. Safari applies the rules privately and tells no one, not even Parapet, what it blocked.
  • The one exception is the YouTube blocker, which is off by default. Only when you turn it on does it see pages, and only on youtube.com: it removes the ad schedule from the video player and sends nothing anywhere. Safari asks for your permission before it runs. While it is on, videos can take a few seconds longer to start.

Rule updates

  • The app connects to one place, its release server on GitHub Pages, to download signed rule lists. It sends nothing but that request. GitHub, which hosts the server, receives the request's IP address as any web server does; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Every release is signed. The app verifies the signature and every file's hash before Safari is switched to it, and again at each launch.

Spyware Threat Analysis

  • Reads only the backup folder, sysdiagnose and Privacy Report you choose; the Mac's sandbox blocks everything else.
  • Your backup password stays in memory for the scan and is cleared afterwards. Everything decrypted is written to one private folder and deleted however the scan ends, or the next time Parapet starts if a crash prevented it.
  • The analysis makes no network connection. It compares your files against indicator lists already downloaded with the rules.
  • The report exists only in the app until you export it, and then only where you save it.
  • History keeps one line per change per area (the date, the area, a headline and counts) for 90 days on your Mac, and one small record per iPhone: the date and salted digests of findings, no device identifier and no finding text, dropped after 90 days. Both can be cleared in the app.

Contacting us

Reporting a broken site opens an email you write and send yourself; it contains only the site's host name. Email you send us is used only to answer you.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details.

Changes and contact
