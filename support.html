Turning the extensions on

  1. Open Parapet once after installing it.
  2. In Safari, choose Safari > Settings > Extensions.
  3. Tick each Parapet extension you want. The YouTube Ad Blocker is off by default; if you turn it on, Safari asks for permission on youtube.com: choose Always Allow on This Website.

Parapet's window shows whether its content blockers are on, and Open Safari Extensions takes you to that Settings page.

A site looks broken

Add the site to Paused Sites in the Parapet app. To tell us, use Report a Broken Site in the app; it opens an email containing only the site's host name.

YouTube

The YouTube Ad Blocker removes video ads on youtube.com in Safari. It is off by default, and it is the only part of Parapet that sees a page. While it is on, videos can take a few seconds longer to start: YouTube delays playback when no ad plays, and that wait is set by YouTube's servers. Ads in the YouTube app are not affected.
