Turning the extensions on

  1. Open Parapet once after installing it.
  2. In Safari, choose Safari > Settings > Extensions.
  3. Tick each Parapet extension you want. Ad Blocker Plus is off by default; if you turn it on, Safari asks for permission on each site it works on: choose Always Allow on This Website.

Parapet's window shows whether its content blockers are on, and Open Safari Extensions takes you to that Settings page.

A site looks broken

Add the site to Paused Sites in the Parapet app. To tell us, use Report a Broken Site in the app; it opens an email containing only the site's host name.

Ad Blocker Plus

Ad Blocker Plus removes video ads on YouTube and stops sites that fight ad blockers from bringing blocked ads back, in Safari. It comes with the unlock, not the free trial. It is off by default, and it is the only part of Parapet that sees a page. While it is on, videos can take a few seconds longer to start: YouTube delays playback when no ad plays, and that wait is set by YouTube's servers. Ads in the YouTube app are not affected.
